Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Plume Game Studio is operated by Plume Group Ltd. This policy covers this website and our iPhone game Corner. Questions go through the support form.

The short version

Corner does not collect, store, transmit, or share any information about you. This website does not use accounts, cookies, or analytics. The only personal details we receive are what you type into the support form.

Corner, the game

Corner has no network code. It cannot send data anywhere. It does not ask for permission to use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, or notifications.

There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, and no analytics. Corner does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA). It does not track you across apps or websites.

The only thing the game saves is your best survival time, in seconds. That number stays in the app’s private storage on your device. It is not sent to us and it is not stored on our servers. Deleting Corner deletes it.

Haptics and sound use the iPhone’s own speaker and vibration. Sound follows the silent switch and does not stop music you are already playing.

Children

Corner is made for all ages and is intended to be rated 4+. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

This website

plumegamestudio.com is a set of static pages. We do not run ads, analytics, or a mailing list, and we do not set cookies.

The company that hosts the site may keep ordinary server logs, such as an IP address, the browser type, and the page requested. Those logs are used to run and protect the site. We do not use them to build a profile of you.

If you use the support form, we receive your name, email address, the game you picked, and your message, so we can reply. We do not add you to a marketing list. We keep the message only as long as we need it to help you.

Changes

If a future version of Corner starts collecting data, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released. We will also update the App Store privacy answers.
