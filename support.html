How to play Corner

Tap the black screen to start. A white square grows from a corner. Tap anywhere to move it to a new corner and make it small again. When the square fills the screen, your time and your best time are shown. Tap to play again.

There is no account. Your best time stays on that iPhone. If you delete the app, the best time is deleted with it. We cannot look it up or restore it.

Corner does not collect play data. Read the privacy policy.
